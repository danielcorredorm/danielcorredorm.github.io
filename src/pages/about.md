I'm a gameplay engineer from Bogotá, Colombia. I have worked in Unreal Engine (UE4 and UE5) since 2021, building gameplay systems in C++ and Blueprints: the Gameplay Ability System, multiplayer replication, combat and enemy AI. My background is a degree in Systems Engineering.

**How I started.** I took my first steps in game development with Unity in 2019; the small games I made then are on [itch.io](https://darkfenix2787.itch.io/). In 2020 I moved to Unreal Engine and C++ with two courses: the first introduced me to the engine and game design, and the second to multiplayer and the Gameplay Ability System. In 2021 I started working in the industry.

## Career in games

- **2025 – present · Indie Level Studio, Senior Gameplay Engineer.** [Mariachi Legends](/games/mariachi-legends/), a 2D pixel-art Metroidvania launching April 2027: GAS enemy and boss behaviors, interactive objects, console-ready gameplay code, code reviews.
- **2023 – 2024 · GAMECAN EU (Estonia), Mid-Level Gameplay Engineer.** [Contenders Arena](/games/contenders-arena/), a third-person multiplayer shooter, and [Oh Hel No!](/games/oh-hel-no/), a multiplayer resource management game: multiplayer abilities (Magnetic Hand), enemy and boss AI, the studio's Unreal documentation and repository guidelines.
- **2021 – 2023 · Teravision Games, Mid-Level Gameplay Engineer.** [Killer Klowns From Outer Space: The Game](/games/killer-klowns/), a shipped multiplayer AA: replicated gameplay with GAS, gameplay tools, multiplayer replication bugs.
- **2021 · ON3D Studios, Junior Gameplay Engineer.** A single-player 3D fighting game: core combat mechanics, tools and gameplay abilities.
