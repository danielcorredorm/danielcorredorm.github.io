## Career in games

- **2023 – 2024 · GAMECAN EU (Estonia), Mid-Level Gameplay Engineer.** [Contenders Arena](/games/contenders-arena/), a third-person multiplayer shooter, and [Oh Hel No!](/games/oh-hel-no/), a multiplayer resource management game: multiplayer abilities, enemy AI, the studio's Unreal documentation and repository guidelines.
- **2021 – 2023 · Teravision Games, Mid-Level Gameplay Engineer.** [Killer Klowns From Outer Space: The Game](/games/killer-klowns/), a shipped multiplayer AA: replicated gameplay with GAS, gameplay tools, multiplayer replication bugs.
